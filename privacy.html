Privacy

Our privacy-first approach

We aim to build tools that respect your data.

How we think about privacy

  • We design apps to be offline-first wherever practical, so files can be processed on your device.
  • We try to avoid collecting data that an app does not need to function.
  • This website is informational only and does not process payments or sell anything.

Each app's own privacy policy is the authoritative source for how that app handles data. This page is a general statement of approach and not a legal document.
